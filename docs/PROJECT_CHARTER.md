# Patient360: Project Charter

## 1. Executive Summary
**Patient360** is an enterprise-grade, evidence-grounded healthcare intelligence platform designed to turn fragmented clinical records (consultation notes, lab narratives, discharge summaries, medication orders) and biomedical research literature into structured, searchable, traceable knowledge.

The platform is strictly an **information synthesis, retrieval, and research platform**—not an autonomous diagnostic tool or clinical decision system. Every factual statement generated by the platform must be directly anchored to an exact passage in an authorized document, complete with page-level provenance.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Modern healthcare data is siloed, heterogeneous, and overwhelmingly unstructured:
* **Fragmentation:** Patient histories are scattered across disjointed PDF discharge summaries, specialist consultation notes, lab results, and imaging narratives.
* **Information Overload:** Clinicians spend excessive time scanning multi-page documents to reconstruct past medical events, adverse reactions, and prior treatment responses.
* **Hallucination Risk in Generic AI:** General-purpose Large Language Models (LLMs) frequently fabricate medical claims, hallucinate dosages, and lack auditable source citations.
* **Lack of Temporal Awareness:** Standard semantic search tools collapse time, confusing a historical diagnosis from 2018 with an active condition in 2024.

---

## 3. Product Vision & Core Pillars

### Pillar A: Patient Document Intelligence
* Ingests heterogeneous clinical documents with guaranteed page-level provenance.
* Extracts searchable text from PDFs and structured formats without destroying clinical units or decimal precision.
* Provides hybrid retrieval (dense vector + sparse BM25) and answers inquiries with explicit claim-to-passage citations.

### Pillar B: Longitudinal Patient History
* Synthesizes clinical events into a structured, chronological timeline.
* Distinguishes the **date of a clinical event** from the **date of document creation/ingestion**.
* Detects and surfaces conflicting facts (e.g., diverging allergy notes, conflicting surgery dates, or mismatched medication dosages) rather than silently choosing one.

### Pillar C: Medical Research Intelligence
* Indexes authorized biomedical literature and clinical guidelines.
* Conducts cross-literature searches and comparative evidence extraction across studies.
* Generates structured **Evidence Comparison Matrices** highlighting study designs, sample sizes, primary endpoints, and documented limitations.

### Pillar D: Bounded Agentic Workflows
* Decomposes complex multi-faceted research inquiries into deterministic sub-tasks.
* Operates under strict bounds: state-machine execution (LangGraph), read-only tool permissions, hard iteration caps (max 5), timeouts (30s), and token budgets.
* Mandates **human-in-the-loop approval** for sensitive research dossier finalization.

---

## 4. Scope and Explicit Non-Goals

### In-Scope
1. Ingestion and indexing of digital clinical PDFs and biomedical research publications.
2. Server-side role-based access control (RBAC) and strict tenant/patient-record isolation.
3. Provenance-preserving hybrid retrieval with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).
4. Automated citation verification and abstention mechanics for unsupported claims.
5. Chronological clinical event extraction and contradiction surfacing.
6. Research study parameter extraction and comparative evidence matrices.
7. Reproducible evaluation benchmark (50 synthetic test items) measuring Recall, MRR, Faithfulness, and Citation Precision.
8. Append-only security audit logging and comprehensive observability.

### Explicit Non-Goals
* **No Autonomous Clinical Diagnosis or Prescribing:** The platform will never formulate diagnostic conclusions or recommend medication dosage adjustments.
* **No Model Fine-Tuning in Phases 1–15:** The platform operates strictly via retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), reranking, and zero-shot/few-shot in-context learning.
* **No Unbounded Agentic Loops:** No open-ended autonomous agents with unconstrained internet access or unmetered tool loops.
* **No Real Protected Health Information (PHI) in Dev/Demo:** Development, testing, and public demonstrations use 100% synthetic or de-identified data.
* **No Replacement for Clinical Judgment:** All outputs include mandatory clinical safety notices and disclaimers.

## 6. Success Criteria & Measurable Outcomes
* **Zero Cross-Patient Data Leakage:** 100% rejection rate for unauthorized cross-patient document queries.
* **Citation Precision:** $\ge 90\%$ of generated citations accurately support the asserted clinical claim.
* **Faithfulness:** $\ge 85\%$ faithfulness on verified synthetic clinical benchmark queries.
* **Abstention Accuracy:** $100\%$ abstention ("Insufficient evidence") when queried about absent information.
* **Performance:** Retrieval P95 latency $< 1.5\text{s}$; end-to-end generation P95 latency $< 4.0\text{s}$ on standard CPU hardware.
* **Reproducibility:** 100% reproducible local development environment operating within 16 GB RAM.

## 7. Governance and Milestone Approval
Development proceeds through fifteen strictly sequential phases. Progression from one phase to the next requires:
1. Complete satisfaction of the 13-Point Definition of Done.
2. Documented test evidence with real execution outputs.
3. Formal review and explicit approval by the Project Sponsor/User.
