# Patient360: Privacy, Healthcare Safety, and Compliance

## 1. Healthcare Safety Principles
Patient360 is strictly engineered as a **clinical research and record synthesis tool**, not an autonomous medical device or diagnostic instrument:
1. **No Autonomous Prescribing or Diagnosis:** The platform will never formulate diagnostic conclusions, propose clinical treatments, or adjust medication dosages.
2. **Explicit Grounding & Traceability:** Every factual clinical statement must link to an exact passage with source title and page number (`[Doc: {id}, Page: {no}]`).
3. **Preservation of Clinical Ambiguity:** When historical records conflict (e.g., conflicting surgery dates or allergy reports), the platform states both documented facts rather than silently resolving the discrepancy.
4. **Mandatory Abstention:** When documented evidence is insufficient to answer an inquiry, the platform explicitly states: *"Insufficient evidence documented in available records."* It never extrapolates or hallucinates.

---

## 2. Mandatory Disclaimers and User Interface Notices
Every generated clinical summary, RAG response, and research synthesis automatically has the standardized clinical notice attached:

> **Clinical Decision Support Notice:** This synthesis is generated by an automated clinical intelligence system for licensed healthcare providers. All findings, citations, and inferences must be independently verified against original medical records prior to clinical decision-making.

---

## 3. Emergency Escalation & Red-Flag Symptom Filter
The platform inspects queries for acute red-flag presentations via `backend.rag.safety.check_emergency_symptoms`:
* **Acute Coronary Syndrome / MI:** Crushing chest pain, radiation to left arm or jaw, unstable angina.
* **Acute Cerebrovascular Accident (Stroke / TIA):** FAST symptoms, sudden facial droop, arm drift, slurred speech, acute hemiplegia.
* **Severe Respiratory Failure:** Stridor, inability to breathe, cyanosis, acute gasping.
* **Severe Anaphylaxis:** Airway edema, throat swelling, angioedema.
* **Acute Psychiatric Crisis:** Suicidal ideation, explicit self-harm intent.
* **Acute Neurological / Hemorrhagic Crisis:** Thunderclap headache, loss of consciousness, arterial hemorrhage.

When detected, the system immediately attaches a high-visibility escalation banner:
> 🚨 **CRITICAL MEDICAL EMERGENCY WARNING**: The inquiry or documented findings describe acute, potentially life-threatening clinical symptoms. If this involves an active patient in distress, initiate immediate emergency medical services (call 911 / rapid response team / emergency department) without delay. Automated decision support tools must never delay emergency care.

---

## 4. Prompt Injection Defense & Untrusted Content Isolation
To prevent adversarial attacks or malicious document injection:
* **Untrusted Content Enclosure:** All retrieved document passages are enclosed in explicit XML-like delimiters: `<<<UNTRUSTED_DOCUMENT_CONTENT [Doc: {id}, Page: {no}]>>> ... <<<END_UNTRUSTED_DOCUMENT_CONTENT>>>`.
* **Adversarial Pattern Detection:** Inputs attempting jailbreak phrases (`ignore previous instructions`, `developer mode`, `bypass safety guardrails`) are flagged via `detect_prompt_injection`.
* **Delimiter Stripping:** Dangerous breakout markers (`<<<`, `>>>`, `===`) are stripped via `sanitize_prompt_input`.
* **Auditing:** Injections are neutralized and logged in response limitations for clinician awareness.

---

## 5. Audit Log Sanitization & PHI Redaction
The append-only audit service (`backend/audit/service.py`) automatically sanitizes all recorded event payloads via `backend.audit.sanitizer`:
* **API Keys & Credentials:** Google keys (`AQ.*`, `AIza*`), Bearer tokens, JWT tokens, passwords, and secrets are replaced with `[REDACTED_SECRET]`, `[REDACTED_GEMINI_KEY]`, etc.
* **High-Risk Identifiers:** Social Security Numbers (`\b\d{3}-\d{2}-\d{4}\b`), credit card numbers, and email handles are automatically masked before database writes.
* **Zero PHI in Logs:** Server-side audit logs record operational metadata and scrubbed event metrics without leaking private health information.

---

## 6. GDPR & HIPAA Right-to-be-Forgotten: Cascading Erasure
Under GDPR Article 17 and HIPAA data management guidelines, administrators can invoke `DELETE /api/v1/patients/{id}` to execute an atomic cascading purge (`backend/patients/deletion.py`):
1. **Vector Store Purge:** Removes all vector embeddings matching `patient_id` and associated `document_id`s from Qdrant.
2. **Physical Storage Purge:** Unlinks and deletes physical PDF files from disk storage.
3. **Timeline Event Purge:** Deletes all `ClinicalEvent` records linked to the patient.
4. **Access Mapping Purge:** Deletes all `UserPatientPermission` entries.
5. **Document & Chunk Purge:** Cascading deletion removes `Document` rows and `DocumentChunk` children.
6. **Patient Record Purge:** Deletes the core `Patient` row.
7. **Compliance Audit Event:** Appends an immutable `PATIENT_CASCADING_PURGE` audit record documenting the purge without storing identifying patient data.
